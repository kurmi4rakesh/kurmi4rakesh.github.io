About

I started as a SQL Server DBA in 2012 and grew into leading teams of up to 25 engineers across nine engagements. The common thread is continuity: keeping critical systems available while everything around them changes, whether that is a move from AWS to Azure, a tenant-to-tenant consolidation, or a handover between outgoing and incoming service teams.

I report to senior manager, DGM and AVP level stakeholders and act as the technical anchor between engineering, clients and programme governance. I work across Waterfall, Agile/Scrum and hybrid methods, and I mentor junior DBAs into leads.

TransferKnowledge transfer between outgoing and incoming teams, with SLA and performance metrics tracked.
